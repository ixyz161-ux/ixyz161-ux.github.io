Privacy Policy for Merge2048: New Challenge

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge2048: New Challenge ("the App"), developed by Tarun, handles information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Merge2048: New Challenge does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

The App does not directly collect or require:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Account credentials
  • Precise location information

The App does not provide user registration, login, or social networking functionality.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

The App may store game-related information locally on your device, such as:

  • Game progress
  • High scores
  • Game settings
  • In-game state or preferences
  • Information necessary to provide purchased or earned in-game functionality

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted by the App to a server operated by the developer.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The App may display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Native advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, information about advertisements displayed or interacted with, and other information necessary for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes.

The information collected by Google may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, depending on applicable settings, consent, and Google's policies.

The developer does not directly receive or store your advertising identifier through the App.

3. Advertising ID

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may use the Android Advertising ID for advertising and measurement purposes.

You may have options to reset or limit the use of your Advertising ID through your Android device settings, subject to the capabilities and policies applicable to your device and region.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Users may purchase digital in-game Keys using Google Play's payment system.

The developer does not directly collect or store your payment card, bank account, UPI, or other payment credentials.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Purchased Keys and Game Data

The App does not maintain a developer-operated server or database that records how many Keys a particular user has purchased or maintains a separate purchase history for the user.

Information relating to the user's in-game Keys may be stored locally as part of the App's game data.

Because this information is not maintained on a developer-operated server, if the App's local data is deleted, reset, corrupted, cleared, or otherwise lost for any reason, the developer may not be able to restore the previously stored Keys or other locally stored game progress.

The developer does not guarantee restoration of locally stored Keys or game progress following events such as:

  • Clearing the App's data
  • Uninstalling and reinstalling the App
  • Device reset or replacement
  • Loss or corruption of local App data
  • Operating-system-related data loss
  • Other circumstances that remove or alter locally stored App data

Users should therefore understand that purchased Keys may not be recoverable from the developer if the corresponding local game data is lost.

Purchases made through Google Play remain subject to Google's purchase records, policies, and applicable restoration mechanisms. However, the developer does not maintain an independent server-side record of the user's purchased Key balance.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

Google AdMob is used to display advertisements in the App.

Google Play Billing

Google Play Billing is used to process optional purchases of digital in-game Keys.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or otherwise directly share personal information collected from users with third parties.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, including Google AdMob and Google Play, may independently collect and process information as necessary to provide their services.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not maintain a developer-operated account or backend system containing user profiles or personal information, the developer does not maintain a database of users' personal information or purchased Key balances.

Game data stored locally on the device is subject to the security of the user's device and operating system.

Third-party services used by the App maintain their own security measures and privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Merge2048: New Challenge is a general-purpose puzzle game and is not specifically designed or marketed as a children's app.

The App does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information.

Advertising and data practices provided by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be subject to additional age-related restrictions, consent requirements, and policies.

9. Permissions

Merge2048: New Challenge does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Phone calls
  • SMS
  • User files or photos

The App primarily requires network access for features such as displaying advertisements and supporting Google Play services.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not operate a server or database that stores users' personal information, user accounts, or purchased Key balances.

Game-related information may remain stored locally on the user's device until it is removed by the user, the operating system, or the App.

Users can generally remove locally stored App data through their Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

If locally stored game data is deleted or lost, the developer may not have the ability to recover the associated game progress or locally stored Keys because the developer does not maintain an independent server-side record of this information.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob or Google Play is subject to those services' respective data retention and deletion policies.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your device, operating system, location, and applicable laws, you may have options to:

  • Reset or limit your Android Advertising ID
  • Manage advertising personalization settings
  • Control permissions granted to applications
  • Uninstall the App
  • Manage Google account and Google Play privacy settings

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or third-party services.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be made available with a revised effective date.
